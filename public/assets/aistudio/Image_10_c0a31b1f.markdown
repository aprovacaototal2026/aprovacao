## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-performance, authoritative, and victorious aesthetic tailored for competitive exam candidates (concurseiros) pursuing high-stakes public careers. The visual personality communicates discipline, victory, uncompromising quality, and elite preparation.

The visual direction combines **Dark Mode Sophistication** with **High-Contrast High-Conversion Accents**:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep obsidian graphite tones (`#0F1115`, `#161920`) create an immersive, distraction-free environment that projects institutional seriousness.
- **Accents:** Vibrant polished gold and energetic amber glow (`#F59E0B`, `#EAB308`) serve as the beacon of victory and focus, driving immediate visual priority to CTAs, progress indicators, and elite badges.
- **Tone:** Confident, structured, tactical, and relentlessly focused on success.

## Colors

The palette leverages a dark hierarchy paired with lustrous metallic gold and amber tones:

- **Primary (`#F59E0B`):** The primary conversion driver. Used on primary action buttons, key highlighted metrics, active progress bars, and critical indicators.
- **Secondary (`#EAB308`):** Bright sunlight amber used for hover states, gradient stops, star ratings, and celebratory completion moments.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706`):** Deep burnished bronze-gold used for subtle borders, active outlines, and gradient anchor points.
- **Neutral Palette:**
  - `Surface Base`: `#0B0D11` (Deep space black canvas).
  - `Surface Container`: `#141721` (Card and modal containers).
  - `Surface Elevated`: `#1D2230` (Dropdowns, tooltips, elevated interactive tiles).
  - `Border Subtle`: `#282F40` (Structural dividers and container borders).
  - `Text High-Contrast`: `#F8FAFC` (Titles, prices, primary copy).
  - `Text Muted`: `#94A3B8` (Metadata, breadcrumbs, descriptions).
- **Functional Semantics:**
  - `Success`: `#10B981` (Gabarito correto, aprovado).
  - `Error / Hot`: `#EF4444` (Erros, urgência, tempo esgotando).
  - `Career Blue`: `#3B82F6` (Badges Carreiras Policiais / Fiscais).

## Typography

The type scale combines **Outfit** for structural headings and tactical impact with **Plus Jakarta Sans** for high-density legibility in long questions, legal excerpts, and study content.

- **Headlines & Display:** Set in uppercase or capitalized weights of **Outfit** (SemiBold to ExtraBold) to convey certainty, directness, and competitive resolve.
- **Body & Paragraphs:** **Plus Jakarta Sans** maintains open counters and clear numeral distinction, essential for timed mock tests, percentages, and dense legislative articles.
- **Numbers & Prices:** Currency symbols, countdown timers, and discount percentages use heavy weight Outfit to ensure clear commercial impact.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adopts a 12-column responsive fluid grid designed for e-commerce catalog discovery, bundle comparisons, and test-taking interfaces.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout, 24px gutters, max-width of 1280px or 1440px with balanced margins for visual comfort.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout, 16px gutters, 24px screen margin.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, 16px screen margins, 12px card gaps.

Component padding enforces rhythmic vertical harmony using multiples of 8px (with 4px sub-tokens for micro-badges and tags).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses dark tonal layering paired with gold luminescence:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - `Level 0 (Base)`: Background canvas `#0B0D11`.
  - `Level 1 (Cards & Rows)`: `#141721` bordered by a 1px stroke of `#282F40`.
  - `Level 2 (Popovers, Flyouts, Sticky Headers)`: `#1D2230` with subtle ambient shadow `0 12px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`.
- **Gold Glow Accents:** High-tier conversion items (Featured bundle, "Comprar Agora" buttons) utilize an ambient drop-shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.35)`.
- **Card Framing:** Premium items use subtle 1px dual-tone gradient borders (`linear-gradient(135deg, #F59E0B 0%, rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.05) 50%, #282F40 100%)`).

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined modern curvature (Rating `2`):
- Standard cards, input fields, and banners use `8px` (`0.5rem`) border-radius.
- Badges, status tags, and pill chips use fully rounded contours (`9999px`) to maintain contrast with the angular product cards.
- Modal dialogs and feature bundles utilize `16px` (`1rem`) to establish distinct visual framing.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Comprar Agora", "Iniciar Simulado"):** Solid linear gradient (`#F59E0B` to `#D97706`), text in `#0B0D11` ExtraBold uppercase. Glowing hover halo and slight scale (`1.02`).
- **Secondary Action ("Ver Detalhes", "Baixar Amostra"):** Transparent background with 1.5px gold border (`#F59E0B`), text in `#F59E0B` SemiBold.
- **Ghost / Neutral:** `#1D2230` background, `#F8FAFC` text, border with `#282F40`.

### Cards (Apostilas, Bancas & Simulados)
- **Structure:** Dark surface `#141721`, top banner with exam logo or cover mockup, floating career badge top-left, and discount tag top-right.
- **Hover State:** Border transitions from neutral slate `#282F40` to luminous amber `#F59E0B`, with card translateY(-4px).
- **Price Block:** Strike-through regular price in `#94A3B8`, prominent current installment or cash price in `#F8FAFC` alongside a glowing yellow PIX discount tag.

### Badges & Tags
- **"Mais Vendido":** Background `#F59E0B`, text `#0B0D11` font-weight 800, star icon prefix.
- **"Atualizado Pós-Edital":** Dark emerald background `#064E3B` with border `#10B981` and text `#6EE7B7`.
- **Carreiras (Policial, Fiscal, Tribunais, Administrativa):** Outlined pill tags with career-specific subtle tinting and `#F8FAFC` text.

### Inputs & Question Radios
- **Search & Checkout Fields:** Dark container `#0F1117`, border `#282F40`, focus state illuminating a 1.5px ring of `#F59E0B`.
- **Question Options (Simulado Engine):** Interactive rows with letter indicator (A, B, C, D, E) in a rounded box. Correct state activates emerald highlights; incorrect state triggers crimson indicators.
